# Eazy88 trend-based TikTok

Lucas (founder of Eazy88, Adelaide) wants one useful, credible TikTok per request. Each video has ONE main objective, chosen before writing: saves, shares, meaningful comments, follows, or qualified App Store visits. Only the last one needs the app; the others give people reasons to follow Eazy88 between the moments they need it. Views alone are not the goal. He does not appear on camera and does not record his voice; a natural AI voice is fine. He dislikes cheap-looking AI content.

**Languages:** explain everything to Lucas in **French**. Voiceover, on-screen text, captions and the public post caption are in **natural English**.

**Never:** publish, schedule, buy credits or subscriptions, or spend money. Do not promise virality, ROI or downloads, and do not invent engagement numbers, search growth, comments or conversion data.

## 1. Rules that protect the brand

- **Only verified features.** Before choosing a feature, re-check https://eazy88.com/en and https://apps.apple.com/au/app/eazy88/id6759782986.
    - As of 31 Oct 2026 these were confirmed: free, no subscription; 88-day tracking with a progress ring; payslip scanning (photo or PDF; the app reads dates, hours and pay, and the user checks the details before saving); jobs, housing, cars, road trips, insurance and map.
    - Listed is not the same as active: before promoting jobs, housing, cars, road trips, insurance or the map, confirm they hold real content today.
    - The app was live on the App Store. Google Play had a button on the website but no listing could be found, so mention the App Store only unless a listing is confirmed.
- **No eligibility or visa claims.** Never imply Eazy88 approves eligible work, guarantees a job, accommodation or visa, or replaces Home Affairs or Fair Work. Don't show the "88 Days Eligible" review screen as if the app certifies eligibility.
- **Facts from official sources.** Verify every visa, pay, tax or employment claim against official pages: immi.homeaffairs.gov.au, fairwork.gov.au, horticulture.fairwork.gov.au, ato.gov.au, abs.gov.au.
    - Say who a rule applies to, for example 417/462 holders who want a 2nd or 3rd year, UK passport holders exempt from specified work, employees rather than ABN contractors, or Horticulture Award workers.
    - Separate *announced/proposed* changes from rules *in force*.
- **No fakes.** No artificial presenters, testimonials, comments, fake news, media logos or invented app screens. Use real app screenshots or recordings with demo data, and label them "Real app screens · demo data". Graphics must be original: illustrations, a Natural Earth map (public domain) or an "EXAMPLE" payslip with no real numbers.
- **Music.** Use only music that is original or licensed for the intended use. Never assume trending sounds are cleared.

## 3. Research (before choosing anything)

Get today's date with the time tool. Prioritise the last 30 days, and the last 7 days for fast-moving trends.

**Topics to cover:**
- arrival and first-week mistakes
- real costs, accommodation and saving money
- finding work, seasonal harvests and moving between regions
- hostel life and solo travel
- road trips, transport and buying a vehicle
- payslips, work records and specified work
- Australian news that affects working holiday makers

Keep this audience separate from hikers and from generic global travellers.

**Where to look:**
- ABC, SBS, The Guardian AU, regional papers and Time Out for news.
- Home Affairs, Fair Work and ABS for facts.
- Reddit and community posts if reachable.
- TikTok Creative Center and tiktok.com usually do not render or are robots-blocked for fetch tools. Try once, then record that as an evidence limitation and move on. A hashtag's lifetime views do not prove current growth in Australia.

**Research table (five angles).** Columns: angle · source URL + publication date + location · observable signal · audience problem · connection to a confirmed Eazy88 feature · status (*verified emerging trend* / *ongoing audience need* / *creative hypothesis*).

**Choosing the angle.** Score each one on audience relevance, practical usefulness, fit with the chosen objective and production effort. Score the product connection only when the objective is qualified App Store visits; that angle needs genuine screens. Evaluate any starting hypothesis Lucas gives, but choose a stronger angle if the evidence supports it.
- Don't force the 88 days.
- Don't make five unrelated news stories.
- If the product connection is weak, keep the topic and leave the product out: a useful video with no app beats a forced bridge.
- Rotate themes and never run two payslip or work-records videos in a row. Test mix: finding and preparing for work · hostel and backpacker life · accommodation and moving · budget and travel decisions · verified Australian news · work records and app demos (at most one in two weeks).

**Worked example (Oct 2026).** The angle was "Heading regional for harvest work? Check three things before you go".
1. Specified work: check the postcode and type of work with Home Affairs.
2. Pay rate in writing: Horticulture Award pieceworkers still get at least the minimum hourly rate.
3. Payslips are due within 1 working day of payday.

The bridge was "keep them in one place", leading to the payslip-scanning demo, with the CTA "add your first payslip". The visa-ballot news (announced 17 Sep 2026, not yet in force) went into the caption rather than the hook. This is the app-visit pattern: use it at most once every two weeks. Other videos end on their own objective.

## 4. Concept, hooks, script

- **Objective and hypothesis.** Name who the video is for (one specific situation), its one promise, its main objective and why the video should earn it. Only for an app-visit objective: name the **activation event**, for example the first payslip saved, and show it with genuine screens.
- **Hooks.** Write three; choose the one that names the audience and the problem within 0–2 s. Promise the answer in the opening title instead of giving all of it away. No vague questions, fear or clickbait. Keep a second one as the **alternative opening** for a controlled test. Only the first ~4 s and the opening title change; everything after is identical.
- **Structure (30–40 s).**
    1. Hook, 0–4 s.
    2. Two or three concrete, accurate takeaways, each with a simple visual.
    3. Only for an app-visit objective: product bridge with genuine screens, about 9 s.
    4. One CTA that matches the objective: save it, send it to someone, answer an easy question from experience, follow (say what future videos will bring), or download. Never stack several.
- **Length.** About 95–105 words fits 35–39 s with Kokoro at speed 1.0.
- **Tone.** Give value before the CTA. No generic intros, fear, fake urgency or unsupported savings.
- **Claims table.** Build one: claim · official source · who it applies to · in force or proposed.
- **Voiceover lines.** Split the script into one clip per caption cue, about 1.5–4.5 s each. Long clips split at their natural comma pause.

## 5. Production pipeline (what worked)

### Voice: Kokoro-82M ONNX (Apache-2.0), `af_heart`
- **Model files.** Get `kokoro-v1.0.int8.onnx` and `voices-v1.0.bin` from `https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/download/model-files-v1.0/` (or `gh release download`). Take `src/kokoro_onnx/config.json` (the vocab) from a `git clone` of the repo. `onnxruntime` is normally preinstalled.
- **Phonemes by hand.** espeak is not available, so write misaki-style US phonemes yourself.
    - Diphthongs are capitals: A = eɪ (day), I = aɪ (my), O = oʊ (go), W = aʊ (now), Y = ɔɪ (boy). ɾ is the flap in "eighty"; ʧ ʤ ʃ θ ð ŋ ɹ are as usual.
    - Put stress ˈ/ˌ before the stressed vowel.
    - Fixed spellings: **Eazy88 = `ˈizi ˈAɾi ˈAt`** · payslip `pˈAslˌɪp` · Australia `ɔstɹˈAljə` · download `dˈWnlˌOd` · free `fɹˈi` · workdays `wˈɜɹkdˌAz` · harvest `hˈɑɹvəst` · regional `ɹˈiʤənəl` · employer `ɛmplˈYəɹ` · PDF `pˈi dˈi ˈɛf`.
- **Voice limits.** No Australian voice is available; say so. Tell Lucas to use TikTok's "AI-generated content" label.

```python
# make_voiceover.py — python3 -I make_voiceover.py model.onnx voices.bin config.json out_dir af_heart vo_lines.json
# vo_lines.json: [["h1","Heading regional for harvest work?","hˈɛdɪŋ ɹˈiʤənəl fəɹ hˈɑɹvəst wˈɜɹk?"], ...]
import json, os, sys, wave, numpy as np, onnxruntime as ort
model, voices, cfg, out, voice, lines = sys.argv[1:7]
SPEED = float(os.environ.get("VO_SPEED", "1.0")); vocab = json.load(open(cfg))["vocab"]
sess = ort.InferenceSession(model, providers=["CPUExecutionProvider"]); names = {i.name: i for i in sess.get_inputs()}
tok = "input_ids" if "input_ids" in names else "tokens"; pack = np.load(voices)[voice]
sdt = np.int32 if "int" in names["speed"].type else np.float32; report = {}
for key, text, ph in json.load(open(lines)):
    assert all(c in vocab for c in ph), (key, [c for c in ph if c not in vocab])
    t = [vocab[c] for c in ph]; style = pack[len(t) - 1].astype(np.float32).reshape(1, -1)
    a = sess.run(None, {tok: np.array([[0, *t, 0]], dtype=np.int64), "style": style, "speed": np.array([SPEED], dtype=sdt)})[0].squeeze()
    idx = np.where(np.abs(a) > 0.01)[0]; a = a[max(0, idx[0] - 600): idx[-1] + 2400] if len(idx) else a
    with wave.open(f"{out}/{key}.wav", "wb") as w:
        w.setnchannels(1); w.setsampwidth(2); w.setframerate(24000); w.writeframes((np.clip(a, -1, 1) * 32767).astype(np.int16).tobytes())
    report[key] = {"text": text, "seconds": round(len(a) / 24000, 2)}
json.dump(report, open(f"{out}/vo_timings.json", "w"), indent=1); print(report)
```

**Sanity-check every clip.** Count syllable peaks on the RMS envelope; the count should come close to the real syllable count. Also find the internal pauses (low-energy runs of at least 90 ms) and use them to split long caption cues.

### Timeline
- Build all timing from the real WAV lengths:
    - first clip at 0.15 s
    - 0.17–0.22 s between clips in a section
    - about 0.4 s between sections
    - about 1.5 s after the CTA
- Store section starts so scenes and highlights follow the voice. For example, tick "Postcode" when the word is spoken.

### Picture: Pillow frames piped to ffmpeg
- **Canvas.** 1080×1920, 30 fps, deep-green background.
    - Header row at y≈226: "EAZY88 · BACKPACKER TIPS" in small caps on the left, wordmark on the right. Progress tiles 1-2-3 sit beside the label during the takeaways.
    - Section head: a big mint number plus a short Bricolage title. Off-white cards with ink text; mint-outlined green chips for the key rule.
    - Footnotes for source and scope at about 27 px, placed right under the content.
- **TikTok safe area.** Keep essentials in x 80–940 and y 220–1380, clear of the top tabs, the right-hand buttons and the bottom caption.
- **Captions.** Burned in, in a fixed band from y≈1206. Use Instrument Sans SemiBold 46 px, white on a dark translucent rounded box, at most 2 lines of about 42 characters. Captions repeat the narration word for word. Write the SRT from the same cue list so both match.
- **Hook.** The title is visible on the very first frame.
- **Real screens.** Use a rounded mask, a soft shadow, crossfades between steps and a mint outline box on the part being described. Never alter values or invent taps. Show a step row such as "Add › Read › Check › Save".
- **Highlight text.** A `*word*` convention in the text renderer colours those words mint. Render glyph by glyph with kerning, display tracking about −3 %.
- **Encoding.** Pipe raw RGB to `ffmpeg -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p -profile:v high -level 4.2`, then mux AAC 192k with `-movflags +faststart`. A 38 s video renders in about 1–2 min.

### Sound
- **Music.** Write an original procedural bed in numpy (no samples): soft chords such as Fmaj7–G6–Em7–Am7 at 92 BPM, a pad, a pluck arpeggio, bass, a soft kick and an off-beat shaker. Or use no music at all.
- **Mix.** music × (0.11 − 0.06 × speech envelope) under the voice, fade out the last 1.2 s, then two-pass `loudnorm` to **−16 LUFS, TP −1.5** (alimiter first).
- **Voice-only file.** Export a separate voice-only WAV normalised the same way.

## 6. QC on the rendered files (before calling anything finished)

- **Specs.** `ffprobe` must show h264 + aac, 1080×1920, 30/1, and the duration in range.
- **Frames.** Pull frames from the **final MP4** with `ffmpeg -ss <t> -frames:v 1` at the hook (0.03 s), every scene, every highlight and the end. Build contact sheets and look at them for:
    - clipped or overlapping text, especially chips overlapping footnotes
    - anything essential outside the safe area
    - captions that don't match the voice
    - a readable CTA
    - invented UI
- **Loudness.** `ebur128` integrated about −16 LUFS. The voice RMS should be 12–17 dB above music-only gaps.
- **Claims.** Re-read every claim against the claims table.
- **Honesty.** Nobody has listened yet, so say the audio was checked by measurement only. A silent export is never a "finished voiced video".

## 8. Evaluation plan (template, in French for Lucas)

- **Organic and paid, separated.**
    - Post only version A organically. Posting A and B at different times confounds hook, timing and audience.
    - Run the clean A/B in TikTok Ads Manager with the creative as the only variable: same audience, budget, dates and objective. Check the minimum budget before launching.
    - Organic is not free: it costs production and posting time.
- **Save, share, comment and follow objectives:** compare with earlier posts of the same format at the same age, at 72 h and 7 days. Use accounts reached as the denominator; track saves, shares, meaningful comments, profile visits and follows that the platform attributes to the post. Don't credit one video with every new follower.
- **App-visit objective, measure in order:**
    1. 2-s and 6-s views, average watch time and completion.
    2. Click-through rate and App Store clicks.
    3. Attributable installs and cost per install.
    4. **Activation:** the first in-app action shown in the demo, its rate (activations ÷ installs) and the cost per activation.
    5. Production time plus ad spend.
- **Tracking needed.**
    - An in-app event for the activation, for example `payslip_saved`; the developer adds it if missing.
    - The TikTok SDK or an attribution tool (AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch) to link ads → installs → events.
    - On iOS, SKAdNetwork data is aggregated, delayed and may be incomplete per creative.
    - For organic, an App Store Connect campaign link in the bio counts only opted-in users. App Store searches after watching are not attributed.
- **Decision.** Compare on cost per activation, then on early retention to explain why. There are no universal thresholds; low volume gives a trend, not proof. Eazy88 is free with no known revenue per user, so assess acquisition and activation efficiency, not financial ROI. Downloads are not revenue.
