# Home Affairs — extraits vérifiés le 7 nov. 2026

Citations exactes, lues directement sur les pages officielles.

## https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/specified-work
- "To be eligible for a second WHM visa, you must have carried out at least 3 months of specified work. '3 months' is taken to mean a period equivalent to the 3 shortest 'calendar' months of the year, that is, a minimum period of 88 calendar days, including weekends or equivalent rest days during your period of employment."
- "To be eligible for a third WHM visa, you must have carried out at least 6 months of specified work on or after 1 July 2019. '6 months' is taken to mean a period equivalent to the 6 shortest 'calendar' months of the year, that is, a minimum period of 179 calendar days, including weekends or equivalent rest days during your period of employment."
- "You do not need to do your 3 or 6 months of specified work all in one go, or all with one employer."

## https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417/second-working-holiday-417
- "UK passport holders can be granted a second Working Holiday (subclass 417) visa without having to meet the 3 months of specified subclass 417 work if they apply on or after 1 July 2024 using their UK passport."
- "Usually, you can only work for the same employer for 6 months."
- "The average processing time is expected to be around three months."

## https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/what-we-do/whm-program/latest-news

Note : ces règles concernent le 417. Les règles du 462 sont similaires mais à vérifier sur les pages 462 avant toute affirmation spécifique.
