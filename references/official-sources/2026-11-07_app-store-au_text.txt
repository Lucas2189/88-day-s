Eazy88 is the all-in-one app for backpackers on a Working Holiday Visa (417/462) in Australia.
TRACK YOUR 88 DAYS
Automatically calculate your validated visa days from your payslips. See your progress in real time with a clear dashboard.
SCAN YOUR PAYSLIPS
Upload your payslips and let AI extract employer, dates, hours and income instantly. No more manual tracking.
FIND JOBS
Browse visa-eligible jobs across Australia — farm work, hospitality, construction and more. Filter by region and industry.
HOUSING & SHAREHOUSES
Find affordable accommodation near your workplace — hostels, sharehouses, and rentals posted by the backpacker community.
CARS & VEHICLES
Buy or sell cars, vans and campervans directly with other backpackers.
ROAD TRIPS
Discover and plan road trips across Australia with routes, stops and estimated costs.
INSURANCE
Compare and find the right health, car and travel insurance for your stay.
MAP
See all listings — jobs, housing, cars — on an interactive map of Australia.
Built by backpackers, for backpackers.
