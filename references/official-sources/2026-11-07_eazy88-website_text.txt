Frequently asked questions
What are the 88 days?
+
To apply for a Second Year Visa (subclass 417 or 462), you must complete 88 days of specified work in regional Australia. This includes sectors like agriculture, mining, construction, and more.
Is Eazy88 really free?
+
Yes, Eazy88 is 100% free to download and use. All core features — day tracking, payslip analysis, and the marketplace — are included at no cost.
How does the AI payslip analysis work?
+
Simply take a photo of your payslip or upload a PDF. Our AI extracts the key information (dates, hours, income, employer) and automatically verifies if the work qualifies toward your 88 days.
Which visa types are supported?
+
Eazy88 supports both the Working Holiday Visa (subclass 417) and the Work and Holiday Visa (subclass 462). The app adapts to the specific requirements of each visa type.
Is my data secure?
+
Absolutely. Sensitive data like TFN and ABN are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. All files are stored with server-side encryption on AWS, and all communications use TLS/HTTPS.
Can I use Eazy88 offline?
+
You can view your tracked progress and calendar offline. Features like payslip analysis and marketplace require an internet connection.
