Looking for a room or a share house in Australia? Check these 4 red flags before you pay anything 👇

1. You can't inspect it: there's an excuse every time, or the room isn't theirs to rent.
2. Money up front: bond or rent before you've seen it, paid in an odd way (money order, pre-loaded card, several different PayIDs).
3. They want your ID: passport or licence before you've even seen the room.
4. The price is too good: very low rent is used to grab your attention.

Before you pay: inspect in person (a drive-by is not enough), check the agent is licensed in your state, search the address, name and email, and keep the listing, messages and payment details.
Already paid? Contact your bank immediately, then report it to Scamwatch.

Source: Scamwatch (scamwatch.gov.au) and ACCC, checked 17 Nov 2026. General information.
