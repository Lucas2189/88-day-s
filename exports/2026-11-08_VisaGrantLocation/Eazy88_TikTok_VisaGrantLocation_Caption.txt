Waiting on your working holiday visa? Don't book that flight yet ✈️

What Home Affairs says (checked 8 Nov 2026):
1. Average processing is expected to be around 3 months for most working holiday applications.
2. You must be in the right place when it's granted: applied in Australia, be in Australia. Applied from overseas, be outside Australia.
3. Wait for the grant before booking flights or locking in work or study plans.

General information, not migration advice. Check immi.homeaffairs.gov.au for updates.
