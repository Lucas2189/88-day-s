## Concept
- **Pour qui :** backpackers qui vont acheter une voiture ou un van d'occasion pour un road trip.
- **Idée unique :** 3 vérifications avant de payer.
- **Objectif :** saves. **CTA :** "Save this for when you're car shopping."
- **Sans l'app.** La rubrique Cars de l'app n'est pas mentionnée (pas encore de vraies annonces).

## Script final (voix off, anglais)
> Buying a car or van for a summer road trip? Do these three checks before you pay.
> One. Ask for the VIN and run a PPSR check. It's two dollars online, and it can flag money that may still be owed, or a stolen or written-off record.
> Two. Never send money before you've seen the car. Fake ads often have a low price, and a seller who can't meet in person.
> Three. Get an independent mechanic to inspect it before you buy.
> Save this for when you're car shopping.
