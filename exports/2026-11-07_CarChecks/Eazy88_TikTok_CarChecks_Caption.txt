Buying a car or van for your road trip? Do these 3 checks before you pay 🚐

1. Ask for the VIN and run a PPSR check ($2 online at ppsr.gov.au)
2. Never send money before you've seen the car (scamwatch.gov.au)
3. Get an independent mechanic to inspect it

Transfer and roadworthy rules depend on the state, so check your state's transport website.
