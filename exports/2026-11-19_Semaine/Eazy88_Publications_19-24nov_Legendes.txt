Paid per bucket, bin or kilo this summer? 🍓 You still have a minimum.

Under the Horticulture Award, pieceworkers have a minimum for each day they work: at least the hourly rate for their level × the hours worked that day.
- Piece rate earns more that day? You get the higher amount.
- A good day can't be used to cover a slow one.
- You should get a written piecework record (task + piece rate) before you start.

Casual level 1 rates from 1 July 2026: $32.18/h (20 and over), $28.96 (19), $25.74 (18). These rights apply whatever your visa.

Source: Fair Work Ombudsman (horticulture.fairwork.gov.au and the Horticulture Award pay guide), checked 17 Nov 2026. For employees under the Horticulture Award. General information, not legal advice.

=== INSTAGRAM CAPTION (carousel, 5 slides) ===
Looking for a room or a share house in Australia? Check these 4 red flags before you pay anything 👇

1. You can't inspect it: there's an excuse every time, or the room isn't theirs to rent.
2. Money up front: bond or rent before you've seen it, paid in an odd way (money order, pre-loaded card, several different PayIDs).
3. They want your ID: passport or licence before you've even seen the room.
4. The price is too good: very low rent is used to grab your attention.

Before you pay: inspect in person (a drive-by is not enough), check the agent is licensed in your state, search the address, name and email, and keep the listing, messages and payment details.
Already paid? Contact your bank immediately, then report it to Scamwatch.

Source: Scamwatch (scamwatch.gov.au) and ACCC, checked 17 Nov 2026. General information.

Just landed in Australia on a working holiday visa? Sort these 3 things before your first shift 👇

1. Tax File Number: free, online, about 20 minutes, apply once you're in Australia (allow up to 28 days). No TFN = tax withheld at the top rate.
2. Ask if your employer is registered for working holiday makers: registered employers withhold 15% on your first $45,000 (2026–27).
3. Super: your employer must pay 12% on top of your wages, backpackers included (18+). You can claim it after you leave Australia and your visa ends (DASP), taxed at 65% for working holiday makers.

Source: ato.gov.au, checked 14 Nov 2026. General information, not tax advice.
