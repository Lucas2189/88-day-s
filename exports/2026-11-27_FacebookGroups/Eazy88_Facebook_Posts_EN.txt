1A · 88 → 120 DAYS
======================================================================
Heads up if you're on a 417 or 462 and planning a second year 👇

Home Affairs announced changes on 17 November:
• From 1 March 2027, a second WHM visa needs 120 days of specified work instead of 88. Work you've already done still counts.
• From 1 March 2027 you'll also need an invitation to apply (a "Next Year Regional Work Guarantee"). Registrations open 1 December 2026, and you can register before you've finished your days. Registering doesn't guarantee an invitation.
• If you finish your 88 days AND lodge a valid application by 28 February 2027, you can still apply the current way, no registration needed.
• Third visa: still 179 days. UK passport holders are exempt from the registration process.

Official page (registration details will be published there): https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/what-we-do/whm-program/latest-news

Not migration advice, just sharing what's on the official site. Happy to answer questions in the comments.

2A · PIECE RATES
======================================================================
Picking fruit on piece rates this summer? Quick reminder of what Fair Work says 🍓

• Under the Horticulture Award, pieceworkers have a minimum for each day they work: at least the hourly rate for their level × the hours worked that day.
• If your piece rate earns more that day, you get the higher amount. A good day can't be used to cover a slow one.
• Your employer should give you a written piecework record (the task and the piece rate) before you start.
• For reference, casual level 1 from 1 July 2026: $32.18/h if you're 20 or over, $28.96 at 19, $25.74 at 18.

These rights apply whatever your visa. Check your own situation with the Fair Work Ombudsman: https://horticulture.fairwork.gov.au

(General info for employees under the Horticulture Award, not legal advice.)

3A · ROOM / SHARE-HOUSE SCAMS
======================================================================
Looking for a room or a share house? 4 red flags before you pay anything (from Scamwatch) 🏠

1. You can't inspect it: there's an excuse every time, or the room isn't theirs to rent.
2. Money up front: bond or rent before you've seen it, paid in an odd way (money order, pre-loaded card, several different PayIDs).
3. They want your ID: passport or licence before you've even seen the room.
4. The price is too good to be true.

Before you pay: inspect in person (a drive-by isn't enough), check the agent is licensed in your state, search the address, name and email, and keep the listing, messages and payment details.
Already paid? Contact your bank straight away, then report it to Scamwatch: https://www.scamwatch.gov.au

4A · FIRST WEEK IN AUSTRALIA (TFN, employer, super)
======================================================================
Just landed on a working holiday visa? 3 things to sort before your first shift 👇

1. Tax File Number: free, online, about 20 minutes, and you apply once you're in Australia (allow up to 28 days). No TFN = tax withheld at the top rate.
2. Ask if your employer is registered for working holiday makers: registered employers withhold 15% on your first $45,000 (2026–27).
3. Super: your employer must pay 12% on top of your wages, backpackers included (18+). You can claim it after you leave Australia and your visa ends (DASP), taxed at 65% for working holiday makers.

Source: ato.gov.au. General information, not tax advice.
