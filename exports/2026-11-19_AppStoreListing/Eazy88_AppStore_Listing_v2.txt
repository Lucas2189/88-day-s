DESCRIPTION (4000 max, needs a new version)  [1169/4000]
======================================================================
Eazy88 helps backpackers on a Working Holiday visa (417 or 462) in Australia track their 88 days and keep every payslip in one place.

TRACK YOUR 88 DAYS
See your progress on a ring and a calendar. No more counting in your notes app or trying to remember which weeks you worked.

ADD A PAYSLIP FROM A PHOTO OR PDF
Take a photo, pick a PDF or choose from your gallery. The app reads the employer, dates, hours and pay for you, and you check the details before saving.

YOUR WORK DOCUMENTS TOGETHER
Payslips, CVs and certifications, stored in one place and ready when you need them.

FREE, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Eazy88 is free to download and use.

COMING SOON
Community listings for jobs, housing, cars and road trips. We're building them now, so these sections may stay quiet for a while.

GOOD TO KNOW
Eazy88 is a record-keeping app. It doesn't decide whether your work counts as specified work for a second or third visa: the Department of Home Affairs does. Check the current rules at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au and keep your original payslips.

Built by backpackers, for backpackers. Found a bug or a day that doesn't look right? Tell us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] so we can fix it.
