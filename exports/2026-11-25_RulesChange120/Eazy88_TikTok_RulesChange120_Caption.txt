It's official: the 2nd-year working holiday visa rules are changing 👇

From 1 March 2027 (subclass 417 and 462 visas, except UK passport holders):
• A second visa needs 120 days of specified work (now 88). Work you've already done still counts.
• You'll need an invitation to apply. Registrations open 1 December 2026, and you can register before you finish your specified work. Registering doesn't guarantee an invitation.
• Finish your 88 days and lodge a valid application by 28 February 2027? You can still use the current process.
• Third visa: still 179 days.

Source: Department of Home Affairs, announced 17 Nov 2026, checked 25 Nov 2026. General information, not migration advice. Registration details will be on immi.homeaffairs.gov.au.
