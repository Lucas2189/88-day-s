Close to 6 months with one employer on a working holiday visa? Your job might be exempt 👇

Exempt right now (Home Affairs, condition 8547, page updated 4 Nov 2026): tourism and hospitality, agriculture, health, aged care and childcare, plus work for the same employer in different locations (6 months max in each).
Not exempt? You need permission from Home Affairs, and it's only given in limited cases.

Checked 8 Nov 2026. Rules can change: check immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. General information, not migration advice.
