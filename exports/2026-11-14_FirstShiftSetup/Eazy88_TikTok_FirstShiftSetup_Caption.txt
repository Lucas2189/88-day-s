Just landed in Australia on a working holiday visa? Sort these 3 things before your first shift 👇

1. Tax File Number: free, online, about 20 minutes, apply once you're in Australia (allow up to 28 days). No TFN = tax withheld at the top rate.
2. Ask if your employer is registered for working holiday makers: registered employers withhold 15% on your first $45,000 (2026–27).
3. Super: your employer must pay 12% on top of your wages, backpackers included (18+). You can claim it after you leave Australia and your visa ends (DASP), taxed at 65% for working holiday makers.

Source: ato.gov.au, checked 14 Nov 2026. General information, not tax advice.
