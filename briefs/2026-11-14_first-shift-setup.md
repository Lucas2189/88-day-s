## Script (voix = sous-titres)
> Just landed in Australia on a working holiday visa? Sort these three things before your first shift.
> One. Get a Tax File Number. It's free, online, about twenty minutes, and you apply once you're in Australia. No TFN? Tax comes out at the top rate.
> Two. Ask if your employer is registered for working holiday makers. Registered employers withhold fifteen percent on your first forty-five thousand dollars.
> Three. Super. Your employer must pay twelve percent on top of your wages, backpackers included. Keep your fund details: you can claim it after you leave Australia. It's taxed, but it's yours.
> Save this for your first week.
